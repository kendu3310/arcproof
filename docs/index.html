arcproof

Paying for something online is a solved problem on Arc. Knowing you got what you paid for is not.

Circle's x402 SDK settles payments on Arc, and it does that well. But search its entire public API for receipt, attest, proof or deliverable and you find nothing. A buyer can prove it paid. It cannot prove what it received.

That gap matters once the buyer is software. A person can open a 3D model and see that it was mangled. An autonomous agent cannot. It gets bytes back, and no way to tell a careful job from a lazy one.

arcproof closes that gap and nothing else. After a service hands back a result, it writes the digests of the exact bytes in and out to a contract on Arc mainnet. The buyer hashes its own copies and compares. No trust in the provider is required at any point — which is the only arrangement that works when nobody is watching.
